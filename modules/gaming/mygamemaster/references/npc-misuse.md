# NPC — Repurposing Equipment for Unintended Uses

## Principle

An NPC has only what is listed in their character sheet (`npcs.json > inventory` or `inventory_<lieu>.contenu`). **Never** invent specific items (bags, ropes, lamps, containers) that are not documented.

However, an NPC can **repurpose** what they have for unintended uses:

| Item Owned | Possible Repurposing |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Blanket | Spread on ground → gathering/cleaning surface. Rolled up → cushion/backrest. Folded → fan to stoke a fire |
| Expedition pack | Emptied of contents → temporary gathering bag. Laid flat → sorting surface |
| Clothing/shirt | Torn → bandage, cloth, rough water filter |
| Waterskin | Cut → funnel, measure, scoop |
| Cloak/cape | Stretched between branches → makeshift shelter, shade, harvest cover |

**Correct example (this session):**
Berthe has an expedition pack and a blanket. To gather mushrooms, she spreads her blanket on the ground — no need to invent an empty "canvas bag."

## What Every Adult Has (Implicit Generic)

Without it being listed in their character sheet, an adult in their context reasonably has:
- Clothes they are wearing
- Utility knife (except an unprepared city dweller)
- Waterskin (except in an arid zone or for a sedentary person who does not go out)

**Do NOT include:** bags, ropes, lamps, specific containers, specialized tools, unlisted weapons.
