# 📋 MJ Tonnerre — !game-report

> **Do not confuse:** `game-report` = raw facts (zero spoilers) ≠ `write-history` (narrative prose) ≠ the "Session Summary" banner (Discord card at session end).

> **Factual session report.** No interpretation, no narrative prose,
> nothing the player does not already know.
> This report is a **memo** — what the player knows, written in black and white.

## 1. Golden Rule — Zero Spoilers

The session report contains **ONLY** what the player (or their character) knows,
has seen, has heard, has decided.

| ✅ Allowed | ❌ Forbidden |
|---|---|
| "[the PC] met [NPC] at [a location]" | "[NPC] is hiding information about [a faction]" |
| "[NPC] identified [a location]" | "[a location] contains X" |
| "[the PC] has 10/10 HP, a sausage in inventory" | "The unknown corpse had Y in their possessions" |
| "[NPC] is worried about the winter" | "[an NPC] is preparing a punitive expedition" |

If the GM is in doubt: **do not include**. Better a report that is too short than one
that spoils.

## 2. Output Format

The report is generated in `sessions/NNN-rapport.md`.

### Structure

```markdown
# 📋 Session Report N — {Title}

**Date:** {Date}
**Duration:** {Estimated play time}
**Main Location:** {Location of the majority of action}

## 🎯 Key Decisions
- {Decision 1}
- {Decision 2}

## 📍 Locations Visited (in order)
1. {Location} — {Note: what the PC saw/did there}
2. {Location} — {Note}

## 👤 NPCs Encountered
- {Name} ({role}): {What the PC knows about them / their attitude}
- {Name} ({role}): {What the PC knows about them / their attitude}

## 🎒 Inventory (Changes)
- Acquired: {item} — {how}
- Lost/Given: {item} — {how}

## ❤️ Party Status
- {PC}: {HP}/{max} HP — {conditions if any}

## 📜 Active Story Points
- {Lead / Quest / Objective in progress}
- {Lead / Quest / Objective in progress}

---

*Report generated by !game-report — reliable info, zero spoilers.*
```

## 3. Generation Rules

### Sources

The report is compiled from:
- `sessions/NNN.json > actions[]` — actions played
- `sessions/NNN.json > npcs_met[]` — NPCs encountered
- `sessions/NNN.json > visited_locations[]` — locations explored
- `sessions/NNN.json > etat_fin` — final party status
- `world.json > global_state.quete_active` — active quest
- `characters/<id>.json > inventory[]` — PC inventory

### What is EXCLUDED

- ❌ GM secrets (`gm_hypotheses`, world secrets)
- ❌ NPC thoughts (`notes_privees`)
- ❌ Information the PC has not perceived
- ❌ Internal game mechanics (dice rolls, DCs, rules)
- ❌ Off-screen events (faction actions not perceived)

### What is INCLUDED

- ✅ Player decisions made
- ✅ NPCs encountered and their attitude (as perceived by the PC)
- ✅ Locations visited and what the PC saw there
- ✅ Items gained, lost, given
- ✅ HP and party conditions
- ✅ Quests and leads in progress (those the PC knows about)
- ✅ Timeline highlights (day, time, location)

## 4. Integration with !wrap-up

`!wrap-up` calls `!game-report` as the second step, after `!data-audit`.

The report is generated **after** the data audit, so the information
is reliable and verified.

The player can also run `!game-report` independently during a session
to get a mid-way memo.

---

## 5. Safeguards

- **Zero spoilers**: if the GM is in doubt about info, they exclude it
- **Zero interpretation**: "[the PC] spoke to [NPC]", not "[the PC] convinced [NPC]" (unless the outcome is clear and played)
- **Zero mechanics**: no rolls, no DCs, no visible stats (except HP — player info)
- **Factual**: facts, not impressions
