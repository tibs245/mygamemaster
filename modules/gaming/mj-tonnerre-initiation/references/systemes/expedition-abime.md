# System "Expedition & Abyss" — Template

d20 system designed for vertical dark fantasy campaigns (like *Made in Abyss*).
Focus: exploration, survival, inexorable descent. Crunch 3/5.

## 🎲 Resolution

- Roll **d20 + modifier** ≥ DC: 10 easy, 15 medium, 20 hard, 25+ heroic
- **5 Stats**: Vigor (STR/CON), Agility (DEX/AGI), Intellect (INT/WIS), Intuition (PER/WIS), Presence (CHA)
- **Skills**: 3-4 specialties per PC, grant **+3** to d20 when applicable
- **Health**: Hit Points + severe wound thresholds (½ max HP = wounded, 0 = unconscious, -max = dead)

## ⚔️ Combat

Simple, fast, lethal. No heavy tactical grid.
- Initiative: Agility, descending order
- Attack: d20 + appropriate stat modifier
- Damage: fixed or roll per weapon (d4 to d12)
- **Flight valued** — survival takes precedence over glory. A successful Agility or Intellect roll breaks engagement.

## 🩸 The Mark of the Abyss

Core mechanic for universes with "layers" or progressive corruption zones.

| Layer | Depth | Descent DC | Effect | Ascent Penalty |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Surface → -100m | 8 | Light vertigo, nausea | 0 |
| 2 | -100m → -500m | 10 | Migraines, insomnia | -2 |
| 3 | -500m → -1500m | 14 | Hallucinations, bleeding | -5 |
| 4 | -1500m → -4000m | 18 | Minor mutations, disorientation | -8 |
| 5 | Beyond | 22 | Loss of humanity | -12 |

**Rules:**
- **Descent**: Roll **Willpower (Presence)** to traverse a layer without trauma
- **Ascent**: Roll **Vigor** with the cumulative penalty of the layer
- Failed descent = +1d3 Fear. Failed ascent = temporary or permanent trauma based on the layer
- Ascending from Layer 5 requires an artifact or narrative sacrifice

## 💎 The Treasure

Each PC defines **a Treasure** — something they refuse to lose.

| Material | Immaterial |
|----------|------------|
| A family medallion | A promise made to a loved one |
| A map leading to legend | The memory of a world before |
| A master's last manuscript | Their own humanity |

**Mechanic:**
- **Once per session**, when the Treasure is threatened or the PC acts FOR their Treasure → **d10 Inspiration** (added to any roll)
- If the Treasure is **lost or destroyed** → permanent trauma, loss of bonus, and potential alignment/personality shift

## 😱 Fear

Gauge 0-10, tracks the psychological impact of the Abyss.

| Level | State | Mechanical Effect |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| 0-3 | Lucid | No penalty |
| 4-6 | Uneasy | -1 penalty to all rolls |
| 7-8 | Terrified | -2 penalty, no concentration (complex spells/skills impossible) |
| 9-10 | Broken | PC flees, freezes, or acts irrationally — **player temporarily loses control** |

**Fear increases:**
- Aberrant creature encountered: +1 to +3
- Inexplicable phenomenon: +2
- An ally falls (unconscious or worse): +2
- Each Abyss layer traversed: +1

**Fear decreases:**
- Secured rest (defensible camp, not in the Abyss): -2
- Act of significant courage: -1
- Ally provides comfort (RP dialogue, Presence roll): -1
- Treasure is protected/safe: -1

## 📋 Character Creation

Standard starting equipment for an Abyss expedition:
- Weapon of choice (d6 or d8)
- Light armor or reinforced clothing (AC 12-14)
- Explorer's pack: 3 rations, 2 torches, 15m of rope, 1 healing kit (2 uses), flint striker
- 1 personal item tied to background
- 1 minor artifact or curiosity (optional — the GM may provide one per PC)

Starting stats: 15, 13, 12, 10, 8 — distribute among the 5 stats.

## 🌍 Inspiration

Designed for: *Made in Abyss*, but adaptable to any universe where vertical exploration and progressive corruption are central — *Darkest Dungeon*, *Hollow Knight*, *The Descent*, *Annihilation*, *Roadside Picnic*.
