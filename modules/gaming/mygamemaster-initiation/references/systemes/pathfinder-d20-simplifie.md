# Simplified Pathfinder d20 System — Template

d20 system inspired by Pathfinder, simplified for narrative and roleplay. 
Focus: kingdom building, political intrigue, exploration. Crunch 2-3/5.
Designed for **Kingmaker**-style campaigns.

## 🎲 Resolution

- Roll of **d20 + modifier** ≥ threshold: 10 Easy, 12 Standard, 15 Difficult, 18 Very difficult, 20 Heroic, 25+ Legendary
- **6 Stats** (Pathfinder standard):
  - **Strength** — Raw physical power, melee combat, lifting/forcing
  - **Dexterity** — Agility, reflexes, stealth, ranged attacks
  - **Constitution** — Endurance, health, resistance
  - **Intelligence** — Knowledge, analysis, history, deduction
  - **Wisdom** — Perception, intuition, survival, empathy
  - **Charisma** — Persuasion, leadership, willpower, presence
- **Trained skills**: bonus of **+3** to d20 when applicable
- **Expert skills** (if applicable): bonus of **+5**

## 🏗️ Kingdom Building (Narrative)

No spreadsheets, no abstract resource points. The kingdom evolves through **visible decisions**:

| Phase | Timeframe | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Camp | 1 evening | First shelter, first fire, first defenses |
| Outpost | ±1 week | Simple building, palisade, well |
| Emerging village | Several weeks | First settlers, craftsman, shop |
| Laws and alliances | Ongoing | Treaties, taxes, justice — decisions matter |
| Crisis and threat | Ongoing | Bandits, creatures, famines, intrigues |

**Rules:**
- **Resources** are narrative: abundant wood, accessible stone, free water — but rare labor
- **Settlers** arrive when the place is both safe and promising
- **Influence** grows when your character's decisions start getting noticed
- An unresolved conflict attracts worse problems

## ⚔️ Combat

Simple, narrative, no grid.
- **Initiative**: Dexterity, descending order (or narrative if no simultaneous combat)
- **Attack**: d20 + Strength or Dexterity modifier
- **Damage**: fixed or weapon roll (d4 to d12)
- **AC**: 10 + Dexterity modifier (+ armor if worn)
- **HP**: 10 + Constitution modifier (level 1)
- No opportunity attacks, no grid — focus is on strategy and choice

## 💬 Social (Diplomacy, Intimidation, Leadership)

System of **narrative grain** rather than fixed threshold:

| Roll | Result |
|-----|--------|
| **< 10** | The NPC becomes defensive, wary, or refuses |
| **10-14** | The NPC listens but isn't convinced — or accepts a minor service |
| **15-19** | The NPC is receptive — open negotiation, honorable offer |
| **20+** | The NPC is impressed or won over to your cause — net advantage |
| **Critical (nat20)** | The NPC becomes a potential ally. The exchange leaves a mark. |
| **Fumble (nat1)** | The NPC feels insulted/manipulated. The relationship is tainted. |

**Reminder:** A critical failure in social can turn a neutral into hostile, or a hostile into a sworn enemy.

## 🔍 Knowledge and Analysis

| Roll | Information Gained |
|-----|-------------|
| **< 10** | Vague, partial, or incorrect info (the GM misleads) |
| **10-14** | Correct but incomplete info |
| **15-19** | Correct info + useful detail |
| **20+** | Complete info + hidden insight |
| **Critical** | Complete info + detail the GM hadn't planned — they add it to their notes |

## 📋 Character Creation

**Starting stats:** 16, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8 — to distribute (standard hero).
Or free distribution (simplified point buy: 22 points, cost 1:1, min 8 max 16).

**Starting equipment (frontier march style):**
- Weapon of choice (if combatant) or trade tool
- Travel pack: 3-5 days of rations, waterskin, flint, blanket
- 15-25 coins/pieces
- 1 personal item tied to background (a letter, a locket, a tool)
- 1 document or information that launches the story

## 🌍 Inspiration

Designed for: *Pathfinder Kingmaker*, but adaptable to any kingdom-building and frontier exploration campaign. Ideal blend for an epic-intimate tone where the character starts with nothing and builds their legacy.
